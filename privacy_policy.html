Privacy Policy

Kaalchakra: The Wheel of Time
Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information the game uses. The developer is Vishvajeet Waichal.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (completed levels, coins, upgrades, outfits, settings such as volume and button layout) on your device only. This information is not sent to the developer or to any server, and the game has no account, sign-in or online profile.

2. Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information through the game.

3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

The game may show rewarded advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure advertisements, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, and may use cookies or similar technologies. This is controlled by Google, not by the developer. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and manage ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads), where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.

4. Purchases

This version of the game does not sell anything for real money.

5. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If advertisements are shown, they are served by Google under its own child-safety and ad-content rules.

6. Data security and retention

Because progress is stored only on your device, it stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

7. Deleting your data

To delete everything the game has saved, open Android Settings > Apps > Kaalchakra > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the game.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game changes (for example, if new features are added). The latest version is always available in the game under Settings > Privacy.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: vishwastudios.support@gmail.com
